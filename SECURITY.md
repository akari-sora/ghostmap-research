# Security policy

Please report suspected bypasses or safety issues privately to the maintainer through a **confidential GitLab issue** when available, or contact the maintainer through their [GitLab profile](https://gitlab.com/aa22396584). Do not attach a functional crash-loop APK, raw sensitive log, or complete reproduction procedure to a public issue.

This repository intentionally excludes executable denial-of-service payloads and construction scripts. The scanner is experimental and its PASS verdict is limited to the documented ZIP checks; it is not a guarantee that an APK is safe to install.
