# Media provenance

`ghostmap-public-demo.mp4` is an edited presentation built from existing Android 16 emulator footage, redacted evidence summaries, and genuine cropped APK Guard screenshots. No Android 17 video or simulated phone footage is presented as a recording. `thumbnail.png` and explanatory title cards are generated graphics, not experiment evidence. The source video and unredacted screenshots remain in the private laboratory.

Captions are available as `subtitles/en.srt` and `subtitles/zh-TW.srt`. The public MP4 has Traditional Chinese subtitles burned into the image from `subtitles/zh-TW.ass`; the SRT files remain separately usable for accessibility.

## Short-video cuts

`bootloop-reel-zh.mp4` and `bootloop-reel-en.mp4` are 33-second 1080×1920 cuts with six step captions (install, reboot, loading screen, crash, same step again, crash loop), made for Instagram Reels and similar 9:16 posts. They use the first 32 seconds of the same Android 16 emulator capture as the demonstration above, with a 1.4-second hold on the first frame. Two limits apply:

- The footage is Android 16 only. The header "Android 16/17 emulator" refers to the overall test result; the Android 17 run was headless.
- The cut ends before the launcher briefly appears. In the full recording the home screen is visible for about 3.7 seconds (≈39.5–43.2 s) before the next `system_server` crash. Caption ⑥ therefore says "stuck in a crash loop", not "never reaches the home screen", and the cut must not be cited as the device never showing the home screen.

The source clip and the caption/render scripts remain in the private laboratory.
